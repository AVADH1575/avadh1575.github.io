Indoor Positioning (UWB & BLE)

Shows where the phone is inside a building, live on the floor plan. The position comes from UWB ranging and trilateration, BLE handles device communication, and step detection with the compass tracks movement.

What I did

  • Built UWB-based positioning: distance ranging to anchors and trilateration logic that calculates X/Y coordinates and positioning angles.
  • Integrated BLE for device communication.
  • Combined UWB positioning with step detection and compass heading to follow the user's movement on the floor plan.
  • Streamed location updates from native code to Flutter through platform channels and showed the live position on the floor plan.
