About

From idea to store release

I'm a Senior Flutter Developer at AMCODR IT Solutions in Ahmedabad. I build cross-platform apps with Flutter and Dart using BLoC/Cubit, Provider and Clean Architecture, and integrate REST and GraphQL APIs, Firebase, Stripe payments and iOS StoreKit subscriptions.

When a feature needs more than Flutter packages can offer, I connect native iOS and Android capabilities through platform channels — that's how I built AR room scanning with ARKit RoomPlan and UWB/BLE indoor positioning.

I've worked on product teams, with clients and remotely with a US-based company, and I own the full app lifecycle: development, debugging, testing, performance and releases to the App Store, Google Play and TestFlight.
